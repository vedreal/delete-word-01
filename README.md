## The word

**ironic** — the one word I would erase from the dictionary forever.

Deleting it is itself ironic: the very word you would need to describe what just happened is the word that is gone. The humour comes from the word itself — its meaning, not shock, not a caption. A reader gets the joke the moment the word is gone, with nothing explained.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–77 characters, exactly one word, nothing attached).
